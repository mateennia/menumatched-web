How do I scan a menu?

From the Scan tab, take a photo of a physical menu, paste a restaurant's website link, scan a QR code, or tap "Quick Scan" on any nearby restaurant. MenuMatched reads the real menu and estimates calories, protein, carbs, and fat for every dish.

Why didn't a restaurant's menu scan work?

Some restaurant websites publish their menu as images only, or don't have a menu page online at all — in those cases, try the Menu Photo option instead and take a picture of the physical menu. If a scan fails unexpectedly, email us the restaurant's name and we'll look into it.

How accurate is the nutrition information?

Every estimate is generated by AI from the menu's own description of each dish, the same way a nutritionist would estimate from a menu without a lab test. Estimated values are always labeled as such in the app, and should be treated as a helpful guide, not exact figures.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → MenuMatched. Cancelling stops future renewals — you keep access through the end of the period you've already paid for.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, food log, saved menus, and favorites, and can't be undone.
