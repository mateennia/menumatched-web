Nutrition estimates

Calorie and macro estimates in MenuMatched are generated by AI from a menu's own description of each dish, not from a lab test or the restaurant's own nutrition data unless otherwise noted. Treat them as a helpful guide, not exact figures — if you have a medical condition that requires precise nutrition tracking, confirm with the restaurant directly.
